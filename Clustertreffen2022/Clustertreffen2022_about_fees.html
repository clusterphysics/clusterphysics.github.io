--- title: "Clustertreffen 2022 - Fees" ---

Fees

Most rooms have double occupancy; there is only a very limited number of single rooms available.

The conference fee includes accomodation, all meals as well as transport between the Leistungszentrum Herzogenhorn and the train station Feldberg-Bärental.

As it is a Clustertreffen tradition, in order to keep the fees for students as low as possible, there is no reduced fee for speakers - everybody pays the same.

Conference fee:

Master/PhD-students 393€
Senior scientists 453€
Senior Scienties single room 523€
There are some hotels in the (not too far) vicinity; for those who
prefer this option the conference fee including lunch and dinner is:
external accomodation 168€

After registration participants will receive an email with payment details.
